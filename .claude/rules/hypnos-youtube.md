# Hypnos: YouTube (sideload builds only, `HYPNOS_YOUTUBE`)

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md`. The build rule (compile all four platforms with and without the flag) stayed there.

**Nothing YouTube compiles unless `YOUTUBE_CONDITION=HYPNOS_YOUTUBE` is set** (`Configuration/Hypnos.xcconfig`; `build-and-sign --set YOUTUBE_CONDITION=HYPNOS_YOUTUBE`, or `EXTRA_BUILD_SETTINGS` in the gitignored `scripts/build-signing.conf`). Other visionOS YouTube players were pulled from the App Store, so until that is settled no YouTube code may reach a submission build. Every file is whole-file gated, and the shared code it touches (tab enums, settings, `MediaSegmentSkipper`, `VideoWindowModel`, `KeychainStore`, `AppLogger`) has `#if HYPNOS_YOUTUBE` cases. **Build all four platforms both with and without the flag before committing.** YouTube lives only in this app: Raven's Native Player and web-yt-dlp's own Apple apps were removed for it (2026-10-09).

The resolver chain (`Services/YouTube/YouTubeResolver.swift`), first match wins:
1. **On the device**: `YouTubeInnertube.player` asks YouTube's player API as its visionOS client (yt-dlp's `visionos` values plus a visitor id scraped from one watch page). The answer is an HLS master up to 2160p60 VP9 HDR, with no PO token and no player JS. Our own `AVPlayer` decodes it once `HypnosApp.init` registers the supplemental VP9 decoder, so the frames reach the depth pipeline. **That registration must cover visionOS**: it was tvOS/macOS-only until 2026-10-25, so the headset refused every VP9 variant (-11848 / -15812), and everything above 1080p and all HDR (VP9-only on YouTube) silently fell to WebKit: no spatial audio, no head lock, and a too-early "still waiting for the server" prompt. There are no ads: those are separate entries in the response, which nothing plays. Its audio is stereo AAC only. Made-for-kids videos and live streams come back unplayable.
2. **The optional backend** (`YouTubeBackend`, web-yt-dlp, `~/Projects/web-yt-dlp`): `/stream?transcode=never&height=1080` for what the device client refuses (age-restricted with its cookies, made-for-kids, live). Apart from watch history it is only this fallback now; the goal is no backend at all.
3. Otherwise the player API's reason, shown in an alert.

**Surround is on the device, from YouTube's TV client** (`YouTubeSurround`, 2026-10-25; the backend's `/api/youtube/audio` is no longer used). Signed out, the `TVHTML5` client (Samsung Tizen values in `YouTubeInnertube.tvAudioFormats`) lists the 5.1 tracks as plain URLs: itag 328 EC-3, 380 AC-3 (and 773 IAMF Opus, unused). `web`/`android` list them SABR-only, and `visionos`/`ios`/`android_vr` list stereo only, which is why the device client never saw them. Getting one to play takes:
- **The `n` challenge**: solved with the player script (`YouTubePlayerScript`: `iframe_api` names the player id, `base.js` is cached per id in Caches, its `signatureTimestamp` goes in the request or the web/TV clients say "The page needs to be reloaded"). yt-dlp's EJS solver runs in JavaScriptCore (`YouTubeSolverScript`, generated by `scripts/update-youtube-solver.sh`; Unlicense + meriyah ISC + astring MIT, in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`): ~1.6–1.9 s on the headset for a new player version, ~0.05 s after (the preprocessed player is kept). Unsolved URLs are 403.
- **The pre-roll**: googlevideo answers 403 until the ad's skip time has passed (yt-dlp's `available_at`, from `adPlacements`), 3–20 s. So the window **starts in stereo** and swaps surround in when it is ready (`startYouTubeSurround` → `playChosenYouTubeRendition(swapping: true)`, the playhead carried), typically 6–8 s after opening.
- **Segments**: the track's `sidx` (`indexRange`) is parsed into ~6 s byte-range segments, served as a VOD media playlist from `LoopbackPlaylistServer` and added to YouTube's master as an audio group by `YouTubeManifestComposer` (stereo variants dropped, so AVPlayer can't pick stereo). EC-3 before AC-3, the default track, no DRC variant. Plain `Range` requests with an AppleCoreMedia UA get 206 once the wait is over.
If the spliced master fails, `dropYouTubeSurround` reopens in stereo at the playhead (`youTubeSurroundRefused`). Settings → YouTube → Surround Sound (`youtube.surround`, default on; reads the legacy `youtube.backend.surround`). Verified on the headset 2026-10-25: 6-channel EC-3, head-tracked spatial audio (`GET videoAudio` on the debug server shows the decoded track and the spatial experience). tvOS (`TVVideoPlayerView`) plays the master in AVKit's own player, so it takes the same spliced master from `YouTubeSurroundSwap.surroundMaster` and the player's coordinator swaps the item at the playhead (re-observing it, carrying the info panel's metadata); a failed surround item goes back to the stereo master where it was. The Mac has the video window itself since 2026-10-25. Checked 2026-10-25 in the tvOS 27 simulator and on the Mac: "Surround 5.1" selected ~6 s in, the playhead carried, both streams buffering. Not yet on a real Apple TV with a 5.1 receiver or AirPods.

**Opening a YouTube video without taps** (DEBUG): `-UITest -UITestDefault youtube.consentGranted=1 -UITestDefault youTubeAutoPlayID=<id or link>` plus `tvInitialTab=YouTube` / `mainInitialTab=YouTube` plays it when the YouTube tab first shows (read once, then removed). For the Mac, build a copy with a throwaway `PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER` and `CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY=-`; launched with `open -n` it showed no window until a second plain `open` of the app (a reopen event).

**HDR** (`YouTubeRenditions`): with `AVPlayer.eligibleForHDRPlayback`, a window plays the master's HDR variants (VP9 Profile 2 PQ), titled " HDR". If HDR fails, `dropYouTubeHDR` replays SDR at the playhead and `noteHDRRefused` remembers the OS version (`youtube.hdrRefusedOnOSVersion`), so a later OS tries again. HDR shows through the system's video layer in `NativeMetalVideoPlayerView` (see `hypnos-video-window.md`).

- **Nothing reaches YouTube or SponsorBlock before a one-time approval** (decided 2026-10-20, like the old demo image source's "Load Sample Images?" wall, but kept). `YouTubeConsent` (`youtube.consentGranted` in UserDefaults) is set only by the tab's `YouTubeConsentWall`. There is no off switch (the user, 2026-10-20: leaving the tab alone is enough), and the YouTube settings stay hidden until then. The services check it themselves too, not only the UI: `YouTubeInnertube` (every call), `YouTubePageSession`/`YouTubeBrowserPage` loads, `SponsorBlock.segments`, and `YouTubeResolver.resolve` (so a shared `hypnos://play` YouTube link or a restored window can't reach out either). The user's own web-yt-dlp backend is not gated. New YouTube network code must check it too.
- **Identity** is `youtube:<id>` (`YouTubeVideoID.videoIdentity`), so depth caches and restoration key on the video. YouTube URLs expire after ~6 h and loopback URLs die with the process. `start()` therefore calls `prepareYouTubeStream` (which also cuts the chosen rendition) before the renderer probe, so a stale URL never reaches it (the probe would route it to WebKit).
- **Picture quality** (`YouTubeQuality`: Auto, 2160p … 144p; the saved preference, `youtube.quality`, set in YouTube settings or by a pick in the video window). **The video window lists the renditions the video actually has and plays the one picked** (2026-10-23, `YouTubeRenditions`): the master's `#EXT-X-STREAM-INF` `RESOLUTION`/`FRAME-RATE` give "1080p60", "720p" (size class by the short side, or a 16:9 picture's height for wider ones); a pick serves a copy of the master with only that class's variants on loopback and swaps it in at the playhead, and a window opens at the highest rendition at or below the preference (the renderer waits for that copy, one master fetch). Copies are remembered with their master (`YouTubeRenditions.source(of:)`): `needsRefresh` follows the master's expiry, `isSurroundStream` asks whether the master was the spliced one, and a popped-out window returns to the master for Auto. The backend's stream isn't a master, so it offers no choice. Auto plays the whole master and leaves it to the player, capped only by 3D load (`youTubeAutoHeight` → `preferredMaximumResolution` via `VideoWindowModel.maximumPictureResolution`); tvOS caps its AVKit player at the saved level. The cap is a square as wide as a 16:9 picture of that height, so a portrait video of the same level fits under it. **The player keeps what it has buffered (a minute or more) at the old size**: lowering the cap also seeks to the playhead, after which the new size shows within about a second (measured on Apple's HLS sample; without the seek it took ~50 s). Auto leaves it to the player until 3D can't keep up (see 3D overload).
- **Subtitles** (`YouTubeCaptions.swift`, 2026-10-27): the visionOS client's player answer lists `captionTracks` as signed `timedtext` URLs that need no PO token or cookies; `fmt=json3` gives the cues, converted to SubRip for the shared parser. Offered: every track a person made, plus the one automatic (`kind: asr`) track, which is in the video's spoken language (flagged `isAutoGenerated`: listed as "(Auto-generated)", never picked by `SubtitlePreference.initialTrack`). **Translations (`tlang`) are deliberately not offered** (the user: machine translation of unreliable recognition; YouTube also 429'd a run of them). The AI Captions entry sits beside them. Cues from auto captions run past the next line, so each ends where the next starts. Not yet checked on a device.
- **SponsorBlock** (`SponsorBlock.swift`, hash-prefix API) segments become `LibraryMediaSegment`s, so both players (video window, tvOS) show them with the existing skip button via `MediaSegmentSkipper.load(videoIdentity:)`. Categories set to auto-skip (Sponsor, by default) skip by themselves, once each, in the video window only (`autoSkipYouTubeSegmentIfNeeded`), announced by the undo button ("Skipped Sponsor · Undo") so a wrongly marked segment can be watched after all. YouTube videos don't loop (`loopsAtEnd`).
- **UI**: `Views/YouTube/YouTubeTabView.swift` (public web search with continuation paging, paste a link, the signed-in feeds below, on-device history in `YouTubeHistory`) on every platform's tab bar. It resolves first, then opens the platform's player (`video-detail` window on visionOS/iOS/macOS, `TVVideoPlayerView` on tvOS). `YouTubeSettingsSection` (account sign-in/out, backend endpoint, token in the keychain, the Surround Sound toggle, per-category SponsorBlock mode) is in all three Settings screens.
- **Signed-in feeds** (Home, Subscriptions, Watch Later, History; decided 2026-10-16, ported from web-yt-dlp's deleted player at `5aae2c7^`). They are **YouTube's mobile site itself**, in a hidden web view, read by a script: no API call carries the account, so its traffic is what a phone's Safari sends. Never "improve" this into `browse` calls with copied cookies.
  - `YouTubeAccount`: the session lives only in a web engine's cookies, a dedicated `WKWebsiteDataStore(forIdentifier:)` (tvOS: `HTTPCookieStorage.shared`, which `UIWebView` uses). Sign-in is Google's page in `YouTubeBrowserView`; passkeys don't work in an app's web view ("Try another way"). Each device signs in for itself: two live copies of one session can sign each other out. Playback stays signed out.
  - `YouTubePageSession` + `YouTubePageScript`: m.youtube.com at a phone's size (iPhone Safari UA), kept in the window by `YouTubePageHost` inside a zero-size clipping container. WebKit only checks that the view is in a visible window. Cards come from each card's `/watch?v=` link; byline is [channel, views, age]. **Load more** scrolls the last `ytm-continuation-item-renderer` into view; the page scrolls `body`, so `window.scrollTo` does nothing. An open dialog (the EU cookie consent) fixes `body`, so the model reports `.needsAnswer` and the browser opens the page for the person to answer.
  - **Nothing may play in the hidden page**: the mobile site autoplays a muted preview of the top card, which can count as a view. `evaluateJavaScript` counts as a tap and unlocks it (measured on macOS), so the app's calls use the SPI `_evaluateJavaScriptWithoutUserGesture:completionHandler:`, and the reader (in the `.defaultClient` content world, invisible to the page) pauses anything that starts.
  - **tvOS** uses the private `UIWebView` (by class name, as tvOS-Browser does), with a 0.5 s poll instead of a message handler. Every `stringByEvaluatingJavaScript` counts as a tap, and HTML5 video crashes it, so `play()` must refuse **before** the page's scripts run. An `NSObject` category method `webView:didCreateJavaScriptContext:forFrame:`, which WebKitLegacy calls as each window is created, installs `legacyDocumentStart`. That script also replaces `IntersectionObserver`: tvOS `UIWebView`'s own one never reports anything coming into view, so without it nothing ever loads more (all measured in the tvOS 26 simulator). Signing in on the TV uses `TVWebCursor`, a Siri Remote pointer.
  - **Tiles** show title, views, channel and the day it was published, one line each. Feeds and search only say "3 days ago", so `YouTubeUploadDates` asks `YouTubeInnertube.publishDate` (a signed-out WEB-client `player` call: no PO token, so it won't play, but its `microformat` has `publishDate`, ~3 KB; the visionOS client's answer has no dates) for tiles on screen only, two at a time, pausing 5 min if YouTube refuses one. Cached in Caches. The day is in the device's time zone, as the user's old userscript (greasyfork 493024, same request) showed it.
  - **Watch history** (decided 2026-10-18): what was watched and where each video was left lives on the YouTube backend (web-yt-dlp `/api/history`, SQLite), **never reported to YouTube** (the user clears YouTube's history often; reporting to YouTube was rejected as distinguishable or costly). `YouTubeWatchHistory` + `YouTubeWatchReporter`: each player (video window, `TVVideoPlayerView`'s `onProgress`) reports start / every 10 s / pause / close with the time seen, through an outbox persisted in UserDefaults. Opening a video resumes at the saved position (≥ 15 s, not within 10 s of the end), asked from the server so another device's progress counts. Tiles draw a red bar from it (or YouTube's own bar on a signed-in page, read by the page script as `progress`). The Recently Watched shelf becomes "Watched", the server's list for all devices. **YouTube's history page is only read**: shown as the History shelf, or loaded once a day in a second hidden page, and posted to `/import` (adds what YouTube's apps watched; never moves a position back; removed videos stay removed). The Firefox extension in web-yt-dlp's `extension/` uses the same API. Off without a configured backend + token. The red-bar selector is unverified signed in.
  - **Working on the Vision Pro, signed in** (the user, 2026-10-16, build `f27e3ba`): sign-in, the hidden page and the signed-in feeds. Not yet checked: tvOS and the TV cursor's typing into Google's form, iOS and macOS sign-in.
- The player API and search are YouTube's private API: every parse is defensive. When it breaks, compare with yt-dlp's `extractor/youtube/_base.py` (`INNERTUBE_CLIENTS`). The visionOS values there were current on 2026-10-09.
- **Not yet verified on the headset**: VP9 4K through `AVPlayerItemVideoOutput` into fake 3D, and PQ input to the depth pipeline. Verified 2026-10-25: 1440p PQ HDR in the native player, on-device 5.1 with head tracking.
